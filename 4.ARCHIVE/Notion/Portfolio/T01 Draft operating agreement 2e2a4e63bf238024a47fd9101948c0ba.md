# OPERATING AGREEMENT

OF

**Digital Herencia  LLC**

This Operating Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into and made effective as of January 1st, 2026, by and between Digital Herencia**, LLC**, a New Mexico limited liability company (the “Company”), and **Ivan P. Roman**, the sole member (the “Member”).

---

## ARTICLE I

FORMATION

### 1.1 Formation

The Company was formed as a limited liability company pursuant to the New Mexico Limited Liability Company Act (the “Act”) by the filing of Articles of Organization with the New Mexico Secretary of State.

### 1.2 Name

The name of the Company is Digital Herencia**, LLC**.

or such other place as the Member may designate.

### 1.4 Registered Agent and Office

The registered agent and registered office of the Company in the State of New Mexico shall be:

**Ivan P. Roman**

**3109 Bosque Road**

**Anthony, NM 88021**

---

## ARTICLE II

PURPOSE

### 2.1 Business Purpose

The purpose of the Company is to engage in **software development, technology services, intellectual property creation and licensing, and any other lawful business activity** permitted under the laws of the State of New Mexico.

### 2.2 Powers

The Company shall have all powers available to a limited liability company under New Mexico law, including the power to acquire, hold, develop, license, sell, and otherwise exploit intellectual property.

---

## ARTICLE III

MEMBERSHIP

### 3.1 Sole Member

The Company shall have one member:

- **Ivan P. Roman** – 100% Membership Interest

### 3.2 Admission of Additional Members

Additional members may be admitted only with the written consent of the Member and upon amendment of this Agreement.

---

## ARTICLE IV

MANAGEMENT

### 4.1 Management Structure

The Company is **member-managed**.

### 4.2 Authority

The Member shall have full and exclusive authority to manage, control, and operate the Company, including but not limited to:

- Entering into contracts
- Incurring liabilities
- Opening bank accounts
- Hiring contractors or employees
- Releasing software under open-source licenses
- Licensing, selling, or assigning intellectual property

No third party shall be required to inquire into the authority of the Member.

---

## ARTICLE V

CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

### 5.1 Initial Contributions

The Member has contributed to the Company:

- **Cash:** $[Amount]
- **Intellectual Property:** As described in Article VIII

### 5.2 No Additional Required Contributions

The Member shall not be required to make additional capital contributions unless voluntarily agreed.

---

## ARTICLE VI

ALLOCATIONS, DISTRIBUTIONS, AND TAXES

### 6.1 Allocations

All profits and losses of the Company shall be allocated **100% to the Member**.

### 6.2 Distributions

Distributions shall be made at the discretion of the Member, subject to applicable law.

### 6.3 Tax Classification

The Company shall be treated as a **disregarded entity** for federal and state income tax purposes unless the Member elects otherwise.

### 6.4 Fiscal Year

The fiscal year of the Company shall be the calendar year.

---

## ARTICLE VII

LIMITATION OF LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

### 7.1 Limited Liability

The Member shall not be personally liable for the debts, obligations, or liabilities of the Company except as required by law.

### 7.2 Indemnification

The Company shall indemnify the Member to the fullest extent permitted under New Mexico law against any claim or liability arising from Company activities, except in cases of fraud, willful misconduct, or knowing violation of law.

---

## ARTICLE VIII

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND OPEN-SOURCE SOFTWARE

### 8.1 Ownership of Work Product

All software, code, documentation, designs, inventions, discoveries, processes, trademarks, and other work product created for or on behalf of the Company (“Work Product”) shall be the **exclusive property of the Company**.

### 8.2 Assignment of Pre-Existing Repositories

The Member hereby **assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest** in and to all software repositories, codebases, and related intellectual property authored by the Member prior to the effective date of this Agreement and published under the MIT License.

### 8.3 Open-Source Licensing

Notwithstanding Company ownership:

- All existing open-source licenses applied to Company code, including MIT licenses, **remain valid and enforceable**
- The Company may continue to release software under MIT or other open-source licenses at the discretion of the Member
- Nothing in this Agreement shall revoke, limit, or conflict with rights previously granted to third parties under open-source licenses

### 8.4 Commercial Use

The Company retains the unrestricted right to commercialize, sublicense, dual-license, or incorporate open-source software into proprietary or hosted offerings.

---

## ARTICLE IX

TRANSFER OF INTERESTS

### 9.1 Restrictions

No membership interest may be transferred without the prior written consent of the Member.

### 9.2 Effect of Unauthorized Transfer

Any attempted transfer in violation of this Agreement shall be void.

---

## ARTICLE X

DISSOLUTION

### 10.1 Dissolution

The Company may be dissolved at any time by written decision of the Member.

### 10.2 Winding Up

Upon dissolution, Company assets shall be applied in the following order:

1. Payment of liabilities
2. Distribution of remaining assets to the Member

### 10.3 Intellectual Property Upon Dissolution

Any remaining intellectual property shall vest in the Member upon dissolution.

---

## ARTICLE XI

MISCELLANEOUS

### 11.1 Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New Mexico.

### 11.2 Amendments

This Agreement may be amended only by written agreement of the Member.

### 11.3 Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire operating agreement of the Company.
