## Enforce meeting → decision → task as the only execution pathway

**Intent**

Meetings do not exist to talk. They exist to emit decisions and work.

### Key Results

- **KR2.1:** All recurring meetings produce at least one of:
    - a decision
    - a task
    - a documented “no-action” outcome
- **KR2.2:** No task is created outside of:
    - Ops meeting
    - Standup
    - Design / Eng / Prod meetings
- **KR2.3:** Tasks generated from meetings include:
    - owner
    - why
    - success criteria
