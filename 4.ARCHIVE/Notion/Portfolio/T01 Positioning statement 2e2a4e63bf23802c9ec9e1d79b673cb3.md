### 1. Answer these three questions about your company.

<aside>

Be descriptive.

**who** are underserved by generic analytics platforms and want an edge in a cutthroat, hyper-local market, **Competitive Advantage** is the *only* locally-built, culturally aware analytics platform **that** translates public sales data and shelf visibility into head-to-head insights that empower strategic stocking, pricing, and product curation.
