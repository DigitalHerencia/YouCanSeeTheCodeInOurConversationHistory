# Digital Herencia Cadence

## Recurring meeting cadence

| Series | Type | Cadence | Primary scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineering Meeting | Engineering | Daily | Engineering team and engineering projects |
| Design Meeting | Design | Daily | Design team and design projects |
| Operations Meeting | Operations | Daily | Operations team and operations projects |
| Daily Standup | Standup | Daily | Cross-team execution |
| Weekly Sync | Weekly Sync | Weekly | Cross-team coordination |
| Sprint Planning | Sprint Planning | Biweekly | Cross-team planning |
| Post-mortem | Post-mortem | Ad Hoc | Incident / outcome review |

The source Meetings database supports four cadence values: `Daily`, `Weekly`, `Biweekly`, and `Ad Hoc`.

## Governance cadence from Procedures SOP

| Event | Timing | Participants |
|---|---|---|
| Milestone Kickoff | Start of milestone | Operations, Product, Engineering |
| Phase Review | End of each phase | All teams |
| Milestone Review | End of milestone | Leadership |
| Retrospective | Post-milestone | All teams |

## Team-specific coordination from Teams SOP

- Operations: weekly leadership sync, monthly financial review, quarterly risk & compliance review
- Product: weekly roadmap review, sprint planning with Engineering, stakeholder demos
- Design: design critiques, Product alignment reviews, Engineering feasibility checks
- Engineering: sprint planning, architecture reviews, incident postmortems
- Marketing: launch planning, campaign retrospectives

These cadences coexist with the live Meetings database. Database series are represented as meeting-series notes and source-derived views; governance cadence remains contract text unless instantiated as a meeting series.
