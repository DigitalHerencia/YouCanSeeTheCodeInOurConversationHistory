# Software Studio Operating Model

## Organizational philosophy

The studio uses functional specialization with enforced handoffs. Each team owns a distinct domain, produces explicit artifacts, hands off verifiable outputs, and is accountable through measurable signals.

## Operations

Mission: ensure the company exists, pays people, and survives contact with reality.

Responsibilities include governance/compliance, budgeting/runway, vendor contracts, payroll/contractor management, and risk management.

Outputs include budgets, hiring approvals, vendor SLAs, financial reports. Handoffs include budget constraints to Product, hiring approvals to Engineering/Design, and compliance requirements to Engineering.

Coordination: weekly leadership sync, monthly financial review, quarterly risk/compliance review.

KPIs: runway, burn vs forecast, vendor cost per user, compliance incidents.

## Product

Mission: decide what should exist and why.

Responsibilities: vision/roadmap, prioritization, requirements, stakeholder alignment, success metrics.

Outputs: PRDs, acceptance criteria, roadmaps, feature-flag definitions. Handoffs: PRDs to Design, scoped tickets to Engineering, release notes to Marketing.

Coordination: weekly roadmap review, sprint planning with Engineering, stakeholder demos.

KPIs: feature adoption, time-to-value, churn by plan, scope accuracy.

## Design

Mission: make complex systems understandable and usable.

Responsibilities: UX architecture, UI systems, accessibility, design tokens, prototyping.

Outputs: Figma files, component specs, interaction notes, UX guidelines. Handoffs: specs to Engineering, rationale to Product.

Coordination: design critiques, Product alignment reviews, Engineering feasibility checks.

KPIs: task success rate, accessibility compliance, design debt.

## Engineering

Mission: turn approved specifications into secure, scalable, production-grade software.

Responsibilities: architecture, implementation, security, performance, testing, reliability.

Outputs: deployed services, versioned APIs, test coverage, observability dashboards. Handoffs: feature readiness to Product, constraints to Product/Design, release builds to Marketing.

Coordination: sprint planning, architecture reviews, incident postmortems.

KPIs: deployment frequency, error rates, performance budgets, security incidents.

## Marketing

Mission: make sure people outside the building know the product exists and want it.

Responsibilities: positioning, messaging, launch strategy, demand generation, brand consistency.

Outputs: campaigns, content assets, sales enablement. Handoffs: messaging to Sales/Ops, feedback to Product.

Coordination: launch planning, campaign retrospectives.

KPIs: CAC, conversion rates, pipeline contribution.

## Cross-team coordination

- Product defines **what**.
- Design defines **how it feels**.
- Engineering defines **how it works**.
- Marketing defines **how it is explained**.
- Operations defines **what is allowed**.
