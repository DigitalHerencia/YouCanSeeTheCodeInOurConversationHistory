# Consolidate source material and establish canonicalization intake

**Priority:** P0  
**Phase:** Source Intake

## Outcome

Collect current and historical Codependent Coding, Hipster Stack, Maximal Template, Loaded Vibes, Codebase Context Utility, and relevant DevNotes material into a provenance-rich source corpus for later reconciliation.

## Checklist

- [ ] Inventory governing and historical architecture/governance material across the relevant repositories and DevNotes.
- [ ] Preserve source provenance: repository, path, revision, observed date, and status.
- [ ] Keep imported/reference material clearly non-canonical until approved.
- [ ] Identify duplicate, contradictory, superseded, and implementation-only material without deleting it.
- [ ] Create a conflict register and reconciliation queue organized by Simple class/responsibility.
- [ ] Record known conflicts, including old workflow-as-domain-logic guidance versus newer workflow-as-composition guidance.
- [ ] Keep owner approval as the canonicalization gate.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Relevant source families are traceably inventoried.
- [ ] Imported material is distinguishable from approved doctrine.
- [ ] Conflicts and obsolete duplication are visible and queued.
