## **Intent**

Operations owns *truth, cadence, and enforcement*. There is one way work enters the system, one way it is reviewed, and one way it is closed.

### Key Results

- **KR1.1:** 100% of active projects are created with:
    - Domain
    - Milestone (M1)
    - Phase (P1.1)
    - Owning team
- **KR1.2:** 100% of tasks are traceable to:
    - a project
    - a indicated meeting of origin
- **KR1.3:** Zero orphan tasks (tasks without a parent project or owner)
