## Introduction

I spent the first quarter of 2026 submitting unsolicited PRs to the markdown files of the most insufferably productive developers on GitHub.

I didn’t touch the code.

I didn’t even fork the project right most of the time.

But I *did* read every line of every `README`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `ABOUT.md`, and `SUPPORT.md`, and I improved them the only way I know how:

with spite.

These are my stories.

---

### **Pull Request: Fix Typo in `README.md`**

**Target Repo:** Primagen’s CLI tool for optimizing sadness using Vim bindings

```diff
- This tool is intended for elite terminal users who want more control and speed.
+ This tool is intended for emotionally unavailable men who think using a mouse is a character flaw.
```

**PR message:**

Hi there! I noticed a small clarification was needed to better represent the user base. Hope this helps!

---

### **Pull Request: Improve Tone in `SUPPORT.md`**

**Target Repo:** Theo’s monorepo that’s somehow also a podcast, a Next.js starter, and an identity crisis

```diff
- If you have an issue, open a discussion or DM me on Twitter.
+ If you have an issue, open a discussion or scream into the void until you convince yourself you're wrong and Theo's right.
```

**PR message:**

Love your work. Just thought we could soften the tone for the emotionally fragile folks out there. Appreciate you!

---

### **Pull Request: Update `CONTRIBUTING.md` for Transparency**

**Target Repo:** Casey’s extremely cursed low-level distributed systems toolkit no one asked for

```diff
- We welcome all contributions.
+ We welcome contributions as long as you enjoy being brutally code-reviewed by someone who thinks empathy is a memory leak.
```

**PR message:**

I wanted to better set expectations for first-time contributors! Thanks for the opportunity 😄

### **Pull Request: Enhance `ABOUT.md` for Clarity**

**Target Repo:** TrashDev’s chaotic fullstack boilerplate that also contains a crypto miner for some reason

```diff
- I’m just a guy who loves shipping and helping others.
+ I’m just a guy who posts 17 TikToks a day and pushes to main at 4am while eating cold ramen off a mechanical keyboard.
```

**PR message:**

Added some missing context for those unfamiliar with your personal brand.

---

### **Pull Request: Fix Formatting in `README.md`**

**Target Repo:** Some absolutely cracked-open TypeScript wizard’s tiny utility library that replaces `if` statements with vibes

```diff
- A tiny lib that helps with boolean logic.
+ A tiny lib that makes your logic harder to follow so you can feel smarter than your coworkers and justify not commenting your code.
```

**PR message:**

Love the minimalism. Just added a little more descriptive accuracy.

### Growth as a Developer

---

Every time I opened a PR, I learned a little more about:

- boundaries
- tone
- how far you can push sarcasm before someone @’s you in a comment and says “lol this is actually funny, merged”
- and most importantly: the fragile ecosystem of ego and markdown that defines the modern web dev experience

---

## Conclusion

---

So if you’re an aspiring dev like me and you’re wondering how to stand out on GitHub in 2026, I have one piece of advice:

**Don’t edit the code.**

Edit the soul of the repo.

The `README.md`.

The `SUPPORT.md`.

The little places where devs accidentally expose their whole personality.

And then… gently, lovingly… roast them.

You’ll either get blocked

or you’ll get hired.

Either way: commit accepted.
