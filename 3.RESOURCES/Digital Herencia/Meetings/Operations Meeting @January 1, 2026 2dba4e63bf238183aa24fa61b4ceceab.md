### Topic #1 — Company-Level Objectives (2026)

**Problem:**

The company currently has execution capacity but lacks a formally constrained, written set of objectives that can govern prioritization, scope control, and task creation.

Without explicit objectives:

- Everything looks important.
- Automation amplifies noise.
- Teams drift.

**Options:**

- **Option 1: Single North-Star Objective**
    - One dominant outcome for 2026.
    - All projects must justify alignment.
    - Risk: oversimplification.
- **Option 2: 3–5 Company Objectives (Recommended)**
    - Clear, bounded objectives with explicit tradeoffs.
    - Enables parallel work without ambiguity.
    - Supports OKR-driven task creation.
- **Option 3: Department-First Objectives**
    - Ops, Eng, Design define independently.
    - Risk: misalignment and local optimization.

**Decision Target:**

Adopt **Option 2**.

---

### Topic #2 — Constraints as First-Class Citizens

**Problem:**

Objectives without constraints lead to burnout, scope creep, and false urgency.

**Discussion Points:**

- Time constraints (weekly capacity, meeting load)
- Cognitive constraints (number of active projects)
- Technical constraints (stack, tooling, maturity)
- Financial constraints (runway realism)

**Outcome:**

Constraints will be written **alongside** objectives, not afterward.
