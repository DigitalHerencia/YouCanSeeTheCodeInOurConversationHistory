# Template Families

These are personal reusable structures extracted from the supplied team-in-a-box material. They support Projects, Areas, Resources, and TaskNotes; they do not create departments or a second task database.

## Families

- [[3.RESOURCES/Template Families/Software Product Development|Software Product Development]] — roadmap, specs, research, experiments, and retrospectives.
- [[3.RESOURCES/Template Families/Design|Design]] — briefs, critiques, research, and design decisions.
- [[3.RESOURCES/Template Families/Operations|Operations]] — SOPs, vendors, reviews, and post-mortems.
- [[3.RESOURCES/Template Families/Product|Product]] — product specs, roadmap, launch, research, and retrospectives.
- [[3.RESOURCES/Template Families/Marketing|Marketing]] — campaigns, editorial planning, launch, and press resources.
- [[3.RESOURCES/Template Families/Engineering|Engineering]] — technical specs, releases, APIs, bugs, and documentation.
- [[3.RESOURCES/Template Families/Startup|Startup]] — founder stages, priorities, and actionable business infrastructure.

Use [[SYSTEM/Templates/Project.template|Project]] as the active-work center and [[SYSTEM/Templates/Task.template|Task]] for TaskNotes records.
