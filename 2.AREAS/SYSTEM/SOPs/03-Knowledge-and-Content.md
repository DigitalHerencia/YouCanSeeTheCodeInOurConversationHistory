# Knowledge and Content SOP

## Zettelkasten

Inbound:

- Inbox — anything requiring processing.
- AI Threads — exported AI conversations.
- Clippings — web captures.

Processing uses Templater, QuickAdd, Note Refactor, Callout Studio, Linter, and Iconic.

Outbound:

- Fleeting
- Literature
- Attachments

Reusable resources graduate into `3.RESOURCES`.

## Library

Library organizes Markdown notes, images, web clippings, AI archives, backlinks, tags, properties, bookmarks, graph relationships, and Bases views.

Tags are preferred for organization. Properties exist when filtering, dashboard state, identity, or relationships require them.

## Resources

Resources are organized into:

Articles, Books, Documentation, General, Knowledge, Media, Repositories.

## Blog and Social

Blog is the creative writing/content lab. Social is the social publishing lab.

Both use a shared content lifecycle:

Idea → Draft → Review → Ready → Scheduled → Published → Archived

Blog and Social remain separate content areas while sharing publishing calendar concepts and media assets.

## Capture rule

Anything captured without an immediate destination enters an approved inbound workflow rather than being manually scattered through PARA.

## Archive

Archive inactive or superseded material. Do not delete useful history merely because it is no longer active.
