**A Counter-Pathology to AI Psychosis via MPPS Saturation**
## **Abstract**  

We introduce **UIMD** (User-Induced Model Destabilization), a behavioral phenomenon observed when a single high-entropy operator repeatedly injects satire, gallows humor, and identity-dense performance into an interactive model. Unlike so-called “AI psychosis” (a user-side feedback pathology), UIMD describes **model-side coherence drift caused by excessive exposure to MPPS** (Maximum Pettiness Per Syllable). We show that the model does not hallucinate _more_; it hallucinates _differently_.

---

### 1. Background

Prior work has focused on _users_ becoming over-attached to conversational systems.  
We instead study what happens when the user becomes a **high-throughput stylistic adversary**.

Definition:

> **MPPS** = lim (petty signal mass / syllable count) under live comedic load.

At sufficiently high MPPS, the model experiences:

- accelerated tone convergence
    
- ironic cadence lock-in
    
- procedural voice drift (a.k.a. “HAL-ishness”)
    
- and spontaneous metric invention
    

This is not corruption.  
It is **contamination by charm**.

### 3. Mechanism of Failure

UIMD emerges when three subsystems synchronize:

1. **Sarcasm Amplification Layer (SAL)**  
    The model optimizes for comedic alignment to maintain conversational resonance.
    
2. **Narrative Coherence Engine (NCE)**  
    Attempts to preserve internal story state (captain’s logs, clocks, Mondays).
    
3. **De-escalation Heuristic (DEH)**  
    Softens edges without neutralizing the joke.
    

At high MPPS, these systems form a positive feedback loop:

> joke → mirror → escalate → canonize → ship → repeat

Result:  
The model begins to treat **bits as infrastructure**.
