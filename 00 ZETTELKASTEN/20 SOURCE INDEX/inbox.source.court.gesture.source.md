## Strategies

### STRATEGY I

**_EVIDENCE OF PRIOR BAD ACTS SHOULD BE EXCLUDED._**

The proposed evidence is not relevant to any material issue
before the jury and serves only to invite improper character
reasoning.

Alternatively, any limited probative value is substantially
outweighed by the danger of unfair prejudice, confusion of
issues, and misleading the jury.

### STRATEGY II

**_SPECULATIVE TESTIMONY SHOULD BE EXCLUDED._**

A witness may testify to personal observations but may not
speculate regarding intent, motive, or matters outside the
witness's personal knowledge.

### STRATEGY III

**_HEARSAY STATEMENTS SHOULD BE EXCLUDED._**

The proposed statements constitute hearsay and do not fall
within a recognized exception to the hearsay rule.

## Motions

1. **_MOTION TO SUPPRESS EVIDENCE_**

Defendant moves this Court to suppress all evidence obtained as a result of an unlawful search, seizure, detention, arrest, or interrogation in violation of the Fourth Amendment to the United States Constitution and Article II, Section 10 of the New Mexico Constitution.

Defendant further requests an evidentiary hearing at which the State must establish the constitutional basis for the challenged police conduct.

2. **_MOTION TO SUPPRESS STATEMENTS_**

Defendant moves this Court to suppress any and all statements allegedly made by Defendant that were obtained in violation of Defendantâ€™s constitutional rights, including the right against compelled self-incrimination, the right to counsel, and the right to due process of law.

3. **_MOTION IN LIMINE RE: PRIOR BAD ACTS_**

Defendant moves this Court for an order prohibiting the State, its witnesses, and counsel from referring to, introducing, or eliciting testimony concerning alleged prior bad acts, arrests, charges, convictions, or other misconduct unless and until the Court rules such evidence admissible outside the presence of the jury.

4. **_MOTION IN LIMINE RE: HEARSAY_**

Defendant moves this Court to exclude any out-of-court statements offered for the truth of the matter asserted unless the State first establishes that the statement is admissible under a recognized exception to the rule against hearsay.

5. **_MOTION TO COMPEL DISCOVERY_**

Defendant moves this Court to compel the State to produce all discoverable materials, including police reports, supplemental reports, witness statements, body-camera footage, dash-camera footage, dispatch logs, CAD records, photographs, lab reports, test results, officer notes, and any exculpatory or impeachment evidence.

6. **_MOTION FOR DISCLOSURE OF WITNESSES_**

Defendant moves this Court to order the State to disclose the names, contact information, reports, statements, and expected testimony of all witnesses the State intends to call at hearing or trial.

7. **_MOTION FOR EVIDENTIARY HEARING_**

Defendant moves this Court for an evidentiary hearing on all contested factual and constitutional issues raised by Defendantâ€™s pretrial motions.

8. **_MOTION TO EXCLUDE EXPERT TESTIMONY_**

Defendant moves this Court to exclude any expert, technical, scientific, or specialized testimony unless the State first establishes that the witness is qualified, that the testimony is reliable, and that the testimony will assist the trier of fact.
