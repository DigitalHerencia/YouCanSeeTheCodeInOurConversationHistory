# A Protocol for Commitment

## The Kernel

***Vouch’s*** doctrine. Cleaned up, tightened, and sharpened:

 ```
Vouch does not ask who is right.
Vouch asks what happened.

 No stories.
 No screenshots.
 No appeals.
 No mediation.
 No subjective judgment.

Vouch is deterministic trust infrastructure. We reduce risk by converting mutual intent into explicit protocol, bounded rules, and economically meaningful confirmation.

Outcome follows system state. No unilateral action can force settlement. No discretionary human decision layer exists to exploit. No ambiguity surface exists for fraud to inhabit.

 If conditions are met, funds move.
 If conditions fail, they do not.
 That’s it.
 ```

## The Capstone

> **Fraud thrives in ambiguity, exceptions, and manual intervention.**                                             **Vouch removes all three.**

That line is especially strong.
Because it defines the enemy:

- ambiguity → conflicting interpretations

- exceptions → loopholes

- manual intervention → corruption / inconsistency / gaming


Remove those:

→ deterministic outcomes
→ lower operational overhead
→ scalable trust
→ clearer user expectations

That’s infrastructure logic, not marketplace logic.
