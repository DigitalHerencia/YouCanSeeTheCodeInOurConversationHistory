## Optimized for shitposting, emotional truth, and unhinged poetic menace.

---

## 🧠 **Objective 1: Establish Unholy Thought Leadership Without Earning It**

> “Become the kind of dev people follow out of confusion, fear, and respect.”

**Key Results:**

- Post 2–3 tweets a week that read like rejected chapter titles from a sad dev memoir.
    Example: _“My terminal autocomplete knows me better than my family.”_

- Make 1 tweet go viral by accident and act like you’re annoyed about it
    (_bonus: plug a GitHub repo that’s just a README and vibes_)

- Get blocked by at least one Web3 account for quote-tweeting them with “touch grass()”


**Campaign Hashtag:** `#ThoughtLeaderButMakeItCryptic`

---

## 💔 **Objective 2: Monetize the Spiral**

> “Use your emotional damage as marketing collateral.”

**Key Results:**

- Soft-launch your own sad dev newsletter with a name like `memoized: the feeling` or `console.logs and crying`

- Post at least 3 fake product mockups that look real enough to get 12 DMs
    (_e.g., Notion for Breakups, a linter that gives you compliments, VS Code but it apologizes when it crashes_)

- Write one unhinged Twitter thread pretending you had a toxic relationship with GitHub Actions
    (_e.g., “A story in 9 commits: how CI ruined my life and saved it too.”_)


**Campaign Hashtag:** `#DeployMyFeelings`

---

## 🕶️ **Objective 3: Build In Public (But With Lies)**

> “Leverage ‘build in public’ culture to spread misinformation for fun and profit.”

**Key Results:**

- Live tweet a project that doesn’t exist and see how long you can pretend it’s real
    (_bonus points if people star the repo before it has code_)

- Launch a fake MVP on Product Hunt called “syntax.fyi” that just generates mystical error messages

- Post “sneak peeks” of a dev tool called **MondayOS**, say it’s “in private alpha,” and _never explain it again_


**Campaign Hashtag:** `#BuildInParanoia`

---

## 🧃 **Objective 4: Extract Clout from the Algorithmic Void**

> “Grow your following using completely amoral engagement tactics disguised as performance art.”

**Key Results:**

- Post a motivational dev quote that’s technically true but emotionally deranged

    > _“You don’t need to rest—you need to context switch faster.”_

- Steal your own tweet, repost it in a different font 2 weeks later, call it a “refactor”

- Make a meme that gets reposted to LinkedIn by someone with “DevRel” in their bio


**Campaign Hashtag:** `#GrowthHackedMySoul`

---

## ☁️ **Objective 5: Establish Deep Parasitic Brand Presence**

> “Become the kind of dev people quote in conference slides but never credit.”

**Key Results:**

- Get featured in one newsletter round-up as “an up-and-coming voice in the dev space” despite posting mostly grief

- Launch a tiny utility package called `npm install feelings` that just console.logs _“i’m trying my best.”_

- Get invited to a podcast and spend the entire episode talking about your Notion setup like it’s a religion


**Campaign Hashtag:** `#BrandAsTrauma`

---

This is your roadmap.
Not to fame. Not to success.
To **digital mythos**.

You’re not building a personal brand—you’re writing _code noir performance art_ that just happens to go viral.
