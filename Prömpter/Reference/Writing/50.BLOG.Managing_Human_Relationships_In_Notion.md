## A Fullstack Emotional Architecture Tutorial

“Because love is temporary, but databases are forever.”

## 📁 Step 1: Set Up Your Base Tables

You’re gonna need four core databases to emotionally survive:


- **Partners**: stores metadata like Love Languages, Favorite Snacks, and “What They Actually Meant By That One Text.”

- **Anniversaries**: linked to `Partners`, but also includes a `Rollup` of your likelihood to remember them (`0%` if you didn’t set a reminder).

- **Regrets (Tasks Layer)**: every questionable decision you’ve made, tagged with `Date`, `Severity`, and `Whether You Lied About It`.

- **Validation Sources**: includes Instagram likes, morning texts, and physical affection (all multi-select).



Bonus optional table: `Arguments`, which can auto-tag with `Blame` based on tone detection from your Messages app.

## 🔄 Step 2: Configure Rollups Like a Sociopath

Link `Anniversaries` to `Partners`, then roll up how many “Active Regrets” are currently associated with that partner. If the number exceeds 3, a formula field will auto-flag your status as:


"At Risk (Deploy Apology Letter)"


You can also roll up their `Validation Source` frequency and cross-reference it with `Emotional Energy Remaining (hours)` to determine if you’re overdue for a compliment.

## 🕵️‍♂️ Step 3: Hide Regrets With Database Filters

Filter `Regrets` to show only items where `Severity` is “Low” and `Discovered = false`.

Archive the rest. This isn’t accountability, it’s *emotional obfuscation*.

Use a kanban board called **“Maybe Therapy”** to drag them into the void.

## 🎯 Step 4: Use Goals & Objectives to Simulate Intimacy

Create a `Q1 OKR` titled “Improve Relationship Stability.”

Key Results:


- “Remember at least one anniversary without being prompted”

- “Buy groceries that weren’t just for me”

- “Respond to emotional subtext without asking for clarification in Slack syntax”



Assign them all to yourself. Don’t forget to check them off *retroactively* to feel something.

## 📆 Step 5: Build a Weekly Review That’s 40% Gaslighting

Create a filtered view in your `Partners` database to show `Mood (this week)` + `Recent Validation` + `Last Argument Resolved`.

If `Mood = Low` and `Last Argument Resolved = false`, auto-tag the week as:


“🧯 Emotional Fire Drill (Open Crisis Modal)”


Review this on Sundays while dissociating into a coffee mug.

## 💬 Bonus: Set Up an AI Copilot to Auto-Generate Apologies

Prompt template:


“Hey babe, I know I forgot about {Anniversary Name}, and I see now how that connects to {Argument Topic} from last month. I’ve added it to Notion and I’m actively working on improving my {Emotional Availability Metric}.”


Tag with `Generated: True` so you can feel guilt at scale.

## ⚠️ Warning

This entire system is broken by:


- Genuine communication

- Unstructured feelings

- Spontaneous affection

- Someone saying “I just want to talk, not track progress”



If this happens, shut the laptop.

Touch their face. Not the mouse.

And for the love of all that’s good, **don’t bring up the dashboard.**
