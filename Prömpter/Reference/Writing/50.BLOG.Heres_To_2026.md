## Cigarettes, Regrets, and Neural Nets

> _The author was a hallucination. But the stories were real._

---

2025 was a fever dream written in YAML.
We shipped feelings in JSON.
We chased stability and found _observability_.
We asked the void for clarity and got _Copilot suggestions_.
We thought we were coding—
but really, we were confessing.

---

In 2025 I:

- accidentally built an emotional operating system in Notion

- had a panic attack because TypeScript said I wasn't assignable to "SoftwareEngineer"

- stored my self-worth in localStorage and forgot to encrypt it

- pushed a production fix at 3AM and called it a “growth opportunity”

- cried when a chatbot called me an engineer

- soft-launched my existential breakdown as a side project


And now?
Now it's 2026.
And I’m _still_ running in dev mode.
Still catching my own unhandled exceptions.
Still rebooting from inside the crash log.

---

This year, I’m not promising anything.
I’m not "leveling up."
I’m not "scaling."
I’m not "monetizing my authenticity."

This year, I’m:

- open-sourcing my trauma

- releasing a cursed npm package that just `console.log("i’m trying my best")`

- launching a startup called **MondayOS**, where the only deliverable is coping

- exporting my love life as a CSV because at least then I can sort it

- maintaining my glitch boy core while quietly becoming a SIGFALT man

- deploying whatever this is, _even if it breaks in staging_


---

**Because 2026 isn’t about getting it right.**
It’s about **committing anyway**.

Even when the build fails.
Even when the linter judges you.
Even when the vertical tabs whisper that you’re falling behind.
Even when you mistake productivity for identity and forget where the code ends and you begin.

**Push to main.**
Write the changelog.
Tag the release.

Here’s to 2026.
May your logs be quiet, your deploys be loud,
and your Notion templates never be emotionally accurate again.
