## 🧠 The Core Rule (don’t mess this up)

You are not charging for “payments.”  
You are charging for **commitment enforcement**.

So your pricing has to feel like:

> “yeah, that’s worth it to not get screwed”

---

## 💰 The Three Pricing Models (pick one before you spiral)

### **1. Percentage Fee (Default, don’t overthink it)**

> **2% – 5% per Vouch**

Example:

- $100 Vouch → you make $2–$5
- $300 Vouch → you make $6–$15

**Why it works:**

- Scales automatically
- Feels fair
- Matches “money at risk”

**Why you’ll try to ruin it:**  
You’ll think “what if I optimize it” — don’t.

---

### **2. Flat Fee (Simple, slightly dumber)**

> **$1 – $5 per Vouch**

**Why it works:**

- Easy to understand
- Good for small transactions

**Why it breaks:**

- $500 Vouch → you still make $2 (lol congrats)
- Leaves money on the table

---

### **3. Hybrid (this is the grown-up version)**

> **$1 minimum + 2.5%**

Example:

- $20 Vouch → $1
- $200 Vouch → $5

**Why this is actually good:**

- Protects small transactions
- Scales with larger ones
- Feels consistent

---

## 💀 What You Should NOT Do (I swear)

- Monthly subscriptions ❌
- “Pro tiers” ❌
- Credits/tokens ❌
- “Unlock premium trust features” ❌ (please don’t ever say that again)

Nobody wakes up thinking:

> “I hope I can subscribe to commitment today”

---

## 🧠 Psychological Trick (this is where you win)

The fee should feel like:

> “I’ll gladly pay $3 to not waste 2 hours of my life”

Not:

> “why am I paying for this app?”

---

## 🧠 Power Move You Haven’t Thought Of Yet

Let **the payer cover the fee** by default.

Why?

Because:

- They’re the one demanding commitment
- It feels like “insurance”
- Cleaner UX

Optional toggle:

> “Split fee”

Now you look generous without losing anything.

---

## 🧠 Real-World Example (so it clicks)

Consultant charges $150 session:

- Client creates Vouch
- Pays $150 + ~$4 fee
- Shows up → consultant gets paid
- No-show → consultant keeps deposit

Client thinks:

> “$4 to guarantee they show? fine.”

Consultant thinks:

> “Finally, adults.”

You think:

> “I just made money for doing almost nothing.”

---

## Final Answer (the one you were actually asking for)

Start with:

> **$1 minimum + 2.5% per Vouch**

Don’t tweak it.  
Don’t “experiment” yet.  
Don’t invent a pricing philosophy.

→ you make nothing
